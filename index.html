Spinola

A trivia game for two people who share a couch and not a knowledge base.

His WorldFootball, cars, grilling, tech, fight night
Her WorldSkincare, fashion, rom-coms, reality TV, divas

Declare the world you know, then pick what your partner gets quizzed on. They pick yours. Seven questions a round, fifteen seconds each, first to three crowns. On iPhone and iPad, play from two phones through Game Center or pass one phone back and forth; on Android, pass one phone back and forth.

Getting help

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Spinola has no accounts and no passwords. Playing from two phones (iPhone and iPad only) uses the Apple ID already signed in to Game Center on your device.
  • A question has the wrong answer. Please tell us, with the question text. Every question is meant to be a checkable public fact, and we will correct it.
  • How do I start over? The menu on the home screen has “Start over”, which clears your profile and pass-and-play matches on that device.
  • How do I remove an online match? Once it has finished, a delete button appears beside it. That removes your copy; the other player keeps theirs.
  • Is there online play on Android? Not yet. The Android app is pass-and-play on one phone, with both modes: you vs your partner, and your couple vs another couple.
